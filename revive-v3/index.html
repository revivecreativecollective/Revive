Hi, I’m Eden.

Visual expression has always been a necessity for me.

Growing up, if my friends and I weren’t sure what to do, we’d grab a camera and have a photoshoot around town or film a music video to whatever song we were obsessed with at the time. I was always the one writing everyone’s Instagram captions, editing their photos, and even helping with their bios. Eventually, people I barely knew started reaching out and asking me to do the same for them.

It’s always been a natural instinct of mine to notice the little things, capture the moment, and turn it into something beautiful and worth sharing.
